# Privacy Policy for Miso

**Last updated: July 5, 2026**

Miso ("the app") is a personal expense-tracking app. This policy explains what
happens to your data when you use it.

## Summary

**Miso does not collect, transmit, or share any of your personal data.**
Everything you enter — transactions, categories, budgets, amounts, notes,
dates — is stored **only on your device**, in a local SQLite database. Miso
has no server, no account system, no analytics, and no advertising.

## What data Miso stores

All of the following stays on your device and is never sent anywhere:

- Transaction amounts, dates, and optional notes
- Category names, icons, and colors you create or edit
- Budget names, targets, and periods
- App settings (currency, language, theme)

## What Miso does NOT do

- Does not require an account or sign-in
- Does not connect to the internet
- Does not use analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs
- Does not share data with third parties, because it never leaves your device
- Does not request access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, or any
  other device permission

## Data deletion

Since all data lives only on your device, uninstalling the app deletes all of
your Miso data permanently. There is no cloud copy to separately delete.

## Backups

If your device backs up app data to iCloud, Google account backup, or a
similar OS-level backup service, Miso's local database may be included in
that backup. This is controlled by your device's operating system, not by
Miso — see your device's backup/restore settings.

## Children's privacy

Miso is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data
from anyone, including children, because it does not collect data at all.

## Changes to this policy

If Miso ever adds a feature that changes this (e.g. optional cloud sync),
this policy will be updated first and the update noted above.
